    Welcome⚓︎

    Introduction⚓︎

    Welcome to my 2024 SANS and CounterHack's Holiday Hack Challenge (HHC) Write-up. Not only is this going to be my first year trying the Holiday Hack Challenge but it is also my first write-up so hang in there with me. While I made it to the capstone challenges of Act 3, I did not complete those final two challenges. While I plan to complete them after the publication of write-ups, I will not be including the write-up for them here.

    The theme of the challenge this year is Snowmaggedon and the challenge is divided into four parts that were released in stages. This was a great format for me personally; while I was unable to publish my write-up on the same schedule, the staged release gave me managable goals for completing the challenges.

    - Prologue: Frosty's Beach
    - Front Yard: Act 1
    - Front Yard: Act 2
    - Front Yard: Act 3

    Spoilers

    The purpose of this write-up is not to just post the answers publicly but to instead document my solution process. That being said, I will be including each of the answers I obtained. If you are going through the challenges, I highly recommend trying on your own before using another's methods. Happy hunting!

    Completion Deadline

    The deadline for completing and submitting a write-up for the HHC competition is 03Jan2025. I was unable to complete the write-up in that time so I will be publishing each section as I finish the write-up.

    Conclusion⚓︎

    Santa

    I thought the holidays were truly lost this year. I am so thankful you were here to right the wrongs of my misguided elves. I will ensure they never jeopardize the holidays again. This is the kind of behavior I expect from Jack Frost and his Trolls, not the elves.

    But, I suppose I have fault in this as well, since it's the first time I've been away at the start of the season, and after last year's unconventional holidays.

    Plus, I didn't inform the elves ahead of time. Quite the lesson learned on my part. Even the best of us can always improve.

    I know each faction had the best interest of the holidays at heart, even if their methods were misguided. It's important to have empathy and forgiveness, especially during the holidays.

    After all, the greatest gift we give AND receive is time spent with loved ones. Never forget that!

    Now let's put all this behind us and be merry. Until next year! Happy Holidays!

    While I was not able to complete the entire challenge, I got so much further than I ever expected and learned so much. This was an incredible experience that I am gratefull for the opportunity to participate in. I am already looking forward to the next time! Big thanks to all the awesome people over at Counter Hack who made this possible.
